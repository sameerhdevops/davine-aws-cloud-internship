## How to read this timeline

- **CONFIRMED** means the available material directly labels or documents the week/activity.
- **PROBABLE** means multiple consistent sources, or a partial label, support the grouping but do not establish every detail.
- **UNIDENTIFIED** means the current local evidence does not establish the assignment. It is not evidence that work was not completed.

The portfolio intentionally has no fabricated pages for unidentified weeks. Supporting screenshots and PDFs remain local because they need security and publication review.
