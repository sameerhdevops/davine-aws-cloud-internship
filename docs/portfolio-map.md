## Deliberate omissions

- No week folders were invented for gaps in the source material.
- No original screenshots or PDFs are published from this repository.
- No application, Terraform, or CloudFormation source is claimed to be present.
- No deployment is claimed merely because a plan, architecture drawing, or report exists.

## Evidence selection policy

The public-facing repository uses original diagrams and documentation. The local, Git-ignored review record retains source-to-section mapping, duplicate findings, and publication concerns without storing credentials.

The [internship timeline](internship-timeline.md) is the authoritative all-weeks record; it includes Weeks 3, 4, 6, and 11 as `UNIDENTIFIED` rather than inventing assignment pages.
